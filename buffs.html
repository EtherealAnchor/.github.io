Расписание События Элизиум Боссы Усиления
Эффекты

Creates a spectral painted entity upon entering a floor. If the deployed character is in the painted area centered around it, she deals 30% more Total DMG. She then triggers a paint explosion that paints enemies with all unlocked colors: they take 6% less DMG initially and 1.8% less DMG for every floor cleared, up to 33% (independent).

Enemies gain a 30% Total DMG reduction. This effect is disabled for 10s after the deployed Valkyrie deals melee DMG or Weapon Skill DMG to them (repeated triggers refresh the duration; independent).

Each time Combo or Charged ATK deals damage, dye nearby enemies black. Such enemies take 30% more damage for 8s (independent).

After dealing DMG to enemies with Ultimate or Combo ATK, the deployed Valkyrie deals 25% more Physical DMG and Elemental DMG for 12s. Repeated triggers refresh the duration.

When receiving a Normal Signet of Decimation or Vicissitude, its level is boosted by 2.
